Privacy Policy

At YTDLPY, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application ("Application"). By using YTDLPY, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

YTDLPY is designed with privacy as a priority. Our application:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not require user accounts or registration
  • Does not track your usage habits or behavior
  • Does not store your download history
  • Does not contain advertisements or trackers

2. Local Data Storage

YTDLPY stores minimal data locally on your device to function properly:

  • Download output folder preferences
  • Format and quality preferences
  • Crash logs (in case of application errors)

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third parties.

3. Crash Logs

In case of application errors, YTDLPY may generate crash logs that are stored locally on your device in the YTDLPY folder. These logs contain technical information about the error to assist with troubleshooting, but do not contain any personal information or browsing history. These logs are not automatically sent to us - you would need to manually share them if seeking technical support.

4. Network Connections

YTDLPY needs to make network connections to download content from online platforms. Our application only makes connections to:

  • The websites from which you are downloading content (e.g., YouTube)
  • GitHub servers (only for checking application updates)

We do not intercept, monitor, or store information about these connections beyond what is required to complete your download request.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal information through our Application, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

7. Open Source

YTDLPY is an open-source application. This means that the entire source code is freely available for inspection. If you have concerns about privacy or security, you or any third party can inspect the code to verify our privacy claims.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at devidrru@hotmail.com.
